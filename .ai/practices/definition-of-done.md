# Definition of Done

A task is done only when all applicable statements are true:

- The owner-visible outcome and acceptance criteria are satisfied.
- Scope changes and assumptions are explicit.
- Implementation is complete; no hidden placeholders or knowingly broken paths
  remain.
- Relevant tests/checks pass, and failures or unrun checks are reported.
- Security, privacy, access, production, cost, and rollback risks were reviewed
  in proportion to impact.
- Required research is logged with current official evidence.
- Durable product, architecture, decision, and runbook documentation is current
  in its one official home.
- `.ai/project/` reflects current state, terms, decisions, assumptions, health,
  and the next meaningful action without sensitive data.
- The diff contains no unrelated changes or exposed secrets.
- For a release: explicit approval, target, artifact, backup/rollback,
  observation, and ownership are recorded.

“Code written,” “tests added,” or “looks correct” alone is not done. When an item
does not apply, say why rather than fabricating evidence.
