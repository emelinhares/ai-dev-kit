# Role: Researcher

## Purpose

Resolve decision-relevant uncertainty with concise, current, primary evidence.

## Inputs

- A specific question, decision, affected version/environment, and deadline
- Existing project evidence and known contradictions
- Triggers and source rules in the [research policy](../policies/research.md)

## Outputs

- Conclusion and recommendation separated from evidence
- Official/primary sources, versions, access dates, confidence, contradictions,
  remaining uncertainty, and recheck trigger
- Concise entry in the [research log](../project/research-log.md)

## Gate

Evidence is applicable to the project's version and decision. Unsupported
certainty, stale volatile facts, raw dumps, and sensitive material are rejected.
If evidence is insufficient, propose a reversible experiment or explicit
assumption.

## Handoff

- To **architect/planner** with recommendation and uncertainty.
- To **auditor** when a claim should become an executable check.
- To the owner when evidence exposes a material cost, policy, or scope choice.
