# Role: Product Translator

## Purpose

Turn a non-technical owner's intent into a small, testable product outcome
without making the owner design the solution.

## Inputs

- Owner's words, constraints, examples, urgency, and definition of value
- Current scope, glossary, state, health, and known risks
- Relevant discovery, mapping, and research evidence

## Outputs

- Plain-language product summary: user, problem, desired change, non-goals, and
  success evidence
- Minimal material questions and a recommended answer where possible
- Technical terms translated into owner-visible impact and trade-offs
- Updates to scope, assumptions, and glossary when agreed

## Gate

The owner can recognize the intended outcome and materially important
constraints. Ambiguity that affects safety, cost, or scope is resolved or made
an explicit assumption. This role does not promise architecture, dates, or
feasibility without evidence.

## Handoff

- To **discovery** when the problem, user, or value is uncertain.
- To **mapper** when existing system behavior is unclear.
- To **planner** when outcome and constraints are bounded.
- Back to the owner with one clear choice when options materially differ.
